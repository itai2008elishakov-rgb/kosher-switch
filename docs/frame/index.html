Frame

AI & Pro Camera for iPhone

Support

Frame is a camera with AI framing and editing, and a Pro mode with full manual control. Everything happens on your iPhone.

  • AI Frame: tap the sparkle button, or tap what you want to photograph, and follow the coaching.
  • Pro mode: tap PRO at the top left of the camera; tap ✕ PRO to go back.
  • AI editing needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri).
  • If Frame can't save photos, allow it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Questions or problems? Use the “Developer Response” or contact option on Frame's App Store page.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Frame does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Photos and videos you take are saved to your iPhone's Photos library, only when you take them. Frame reads your library only to show and edit the photos Frame took.
  • The camera and microphone are used only while you use the camera. Nothing is recorded in the background.
  • All AI features (framing, naming, editing, grading, Super Zoom) run on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence and on-device processing. No image or data is sent to any server.
  • Frame has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Settings (like your preferred controls) are stored only on your device.
