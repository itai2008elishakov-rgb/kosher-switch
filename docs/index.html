Who it's for

For your child. For yourself.

A kosher phone works best when the person using it doesn't hold the key. Kosher Switch is built around that: one person sets the code, the other gets a calm, kosher phone.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parents

Give your child a phone without giving them the internet. You choose the apps and the code; they get calls, messages, the camera, a siddur and a luach.

  • Only you can leave kosher mode
  • Can't be deleted, not even after a restart
  • On iPhone, Family Sharing makes you the only one who can remove it

Setup

Two ways to set it up

Kosher Switch has to be the phone's "device owner". Android allows that only through one of these.

A. With this QR code

No computer · the phone is reset
  1. Back up the phone, then factory reset it (or start with a new phone).
  2. On the very first Welcome screen, tap the screen 6 times.
  3. Connect to Wi-Fi if asked, then scan this code:

The phone downloads and sets up Kosher Switch by itself. Needs a phone with Google's setup screen.

B. With a computer

Keeps everything on the phone
  1. Install the app with the Download button.
  2. Settings → About phone → Software information → tap Build number 7 times.
  3. Developer options → turn on USB debugging.
  4. Settings → Accounts → remove all accounts for a moment.
  5. Connect to a computer with Android platform tools and run: adb shell dpm set-device-owner dev.kosherswitch/.KosherAdmin
  6. Add your accounts back.
Questions

Good to know

Is Kosher Switch a kosher filter?

It works like a kosher filter for the whole phone. In kosher mode only the apps you choose are visible, the internet is blocked except for apps you allow (and those still go through a family filter with SafeSearch), and nothing can be installed, removed or changed without the code. Unlike a browser filter, it can't be bypassed by another app or a different browser.

Can it cover pictures of people, like a kosher computer filter?

Yes, in the Kosher Browser (new in 1.3.2). Every picture is checked on the phone before it appears, and any picture with a person in it stays covered, even on Google Images. Pictures without people, like nature, food and maps, still show. While website pictures are off, the Google app and other browsers stay offline, so searching happens in the Kosher Browser.

האם אפשר להסתיר תמונות של אנשים, כמו בסינון למחשב?

כן, בדפדפן הכשר (חדש בגרסה 1.3.2). כל תמונה נבדקת בטלפון לפני שהיא מופיעה, וכל תמונה שיש בה אדם נשארת מכוסה, גם בחיפוש תמונות של גוגל. תמונות בלי אנשים, כמו נוף, אוכל ומפות, מוצגות כרגיל. כשתמונות באתרים כבויות, אפליקציית גוגל ודפדפנים אחרים נשארים בלי אינטרנט, והחיפוש נעשה בדפדפן הכשר.

How do I make my Android phone kosher?

Install Kosher Switch with the QR code or a computer (one time), choose the apps and a code, and tap the Kosher Switch tile. The phone becomes a kosher phone instantly, and only the code turns it back. It works on Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi and most Android phones.

Is there a kosher filter for Samsung phones?

Yes. Kosher Switch works on Samsung Galaxy phones with Android 10 or newer, including the A series and S series, and on keypad phones like the Qin.

מה זה Kosher Switch?

אפליקציה שהופכת טלפון אנדרואיד או אייפון רגיל לטלפון כשר בלחיצה אחת, ובחזרה רק עם קוד. מתאים להורים שמכינים טלפון לילד, ולמי שרוצה טלפון כשר לעצמו עם אדם שהוא סומך עליו שמחזיק את הקוד. כולל סידור ותהילים, לוח ושעות היום, ועוזר קולי שעובד בלי אינטרנט.

איך הופכים טלפון רגיל לטלפון כשר?

מתקינים את Kosher Switch פעם אחת (עם קוד QR או מחשב), בוחרים אילו אפליקציות מותרות וקוד, ולוחצים על המתג. במצב כשר רואים רק את האפליקציות שנבחרו, האינטרנט חסום חוץ ממה שמותר, ואי אפשר להתקין או למחוק אפליקציות. זה עובד כמו סינון כשר לכל הטלפון.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no accounts, no subscription. The code is public on GitHub.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes, it's coming soon. It isn't on the App Store yet. It's built on Apple's Screen Time, so it needs no computer and no reset. iPhone doesn't allow any app to replace the Home Screen or block the Phone and Settings apps, so on iPhone Kosher Switch gives you a kosher Home Screen made of widgets and a Kosher Focus instead.

I want it for myself. Who should hold the code?

Anyone you trust and see often: a spouse, a parent, your rav or a chavruta. Install Kosher Switch, hand them the phone at the "choose a code" step, and let them type it without you watching. When you need your full phone, ask them.

Why does setup need a QR code or a computer?

Android only lets an app lock a phone if it's made the "device owner" in a special, one-time way. That rule protects everyone from malicious apps. Every parental-control and kosher-filter app on Android works like this.

Can someone get around kosher mode?

Kosher mode blocks the Settings app, installing and uninstalling, account changes, VPN and DNS changes, factory reset from Settings, safe mode and USB debugging, and it survives restarts. A full factory reset with the hardware buttons can't be blocked by any app, but it erases the whole phone, so it can't happen unnoticed. No phone is 100% bypass-proof; this is a strong lock, not a guarantee.

Does the assistant need internet?

No. It runs completely on the phone. Its AI part is an optional 1.6 GB download over Wi-Fi, done once in open mode. Nothing you ask is sent anywhere. It's a small model, so answers are simpler than online AI, and recipes get an automatic kashrut check.

Which phones does it work on?

Any Android phone with Android 10 or newer and a 64-bit processor: Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi and Redmi, keypad phones like the Qin F21 Pro, Motorola, OnePlus and others. It's fully tested on the Samsung Galaxy A32 5G (Android 12), with more phones being tested. QR setup also needs Google's setup screen.

Why does it need Developer options, when other apps don't?

Most apps only control themselves. Kosher Switch controls the whole phone: it hides other apps, cuts their internet and locks Settings. Android gives that "master key" only through a special one-time setup, so no downloaded app (or virus) can lock a phone on its own. The setup proves the phone's owner really wants it. Every parental-control and kosher-filter app on Android has the same step.

Is this a kashrut certification?

No. Kosher Switch is a tool. For a certified kosher phone, speak to your rav or a recognized kashrut organization. Parents remain responsible for how a phone is used.

How do I remove it?

On Android it protects itself as device owner, so removing Kosher Switch requires a factory reset. Back up the phone first. On iPhone, leave kosher mode with the code and delete it like any app; if a parent set it up through Family Sharing, the parent has to approve.
