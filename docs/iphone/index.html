Kosher Switch for iPhone

A kosher iPhone, built on Apple's Screen Time

No computer and no reset. Kosher Switch uses Apple's own Screen Time controls, so blocked apps disappear from the Home Screen and websites are blocked everywhere, even inside apps.

🌐

Our advice: no browser. Websites start fully blocked, Safari included. We recommend keeping it that way; a filter is there if you really need one. The choice is yours.

  • A kosher Home Screen like a minimalist phone: a big clock, the Hebrew date and your apps as a calm list
  • The כשר button in Control Center, and a Kosher Focus with its own wallpaper
  • Up to 50 apps you choose disappear from the Home Screen; every other app shows the kosher screen
  • Websites: all blocked by default (our advice), or a kosher filter that blocks adult sites everywhere
  • Can't be deleted in kosher mode; no installing apps, accounts and passcode locked
  • With Family Sharing, only the parent's Apple Account can remove it
  • Siddur, luach & zmanim, notes with drawing, and the assistant, inside the app
  • Apple's Weather, Photos, Camera and Maps stay available if you allow them

How it works

Four steps, all in the app

Allow Screen Time

Once, with Face ID. For a child's iPhone in Family Sharing, the parent approves with their own Apple Account.

Keep & hide

Choose the apps you keep, and up to 50 apps that disappear from the Home Screen. Everything else shows the kosher screen.

Choose a code

Let the person you trust type it. Only that code turns kosher mode off.

Tap כשר

In the app or in Control Center. A Kosher Focus can switch the wallpaper and Home Screen with it.

Privacy & support

Nothing leaves your phone

Kosher Switch for iPhone collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

  • Screen Time: the apps you choose are stored by Apple as private tokens; Kosher Switch can't even see their names.
  • Location is used only on the phone, to calculate zmanim.
  • Contacts are read only when you ask the assistant to call or message someone.
  • The assistant uses Apple's on-device model; your questions never leave the phone.

Questions or problems? Open an issue on GitHub.

Kosher Switch is not a kashrut certification. Parents remain responsible for how a phone is used.
