Screens

Made to feel calm.

Kosher wallpaper

Give the Kosher Focus its own look.

iPhone doesn't let apps change the wallpaper, so we made one for you: deep blue with the gold Kosher Switch seal, placed low so the clock stays clear. Everyone can see at a glance that the phone is kosher.

  1. Download it on your iPhone and tap Save to Photos (or in the app: Kosher Home Screen → Save the wallpaper).
  2. Settings → Focus → Kosher → Customize Screens → choose it for the Lock Screen.

How it works

Four steps, all in the app.

Allow Screen Time

Once, with Face ID. For a child's iPhone in Family Sharing, the parent approves with their own Apple Account.

Keep & hide

Choose the apps you keep, and up to 50 apps that disappear from the Home Screen. Everything else shows the kosher screen.

Choose a code

Let the person you trust type it. Only that code turns kosher mode off.

Tap כשר

In the app or in Control Center. A Kosher Focus can switch the wallpaper and Home Screen with it.

Privacy & support

Nothing leaves your phone.

Kosher Switch for iPhone collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

  • Screen Time: the apps you choose are stored by Apple as private tokens; Kosher Switch can't even see their names.
  • Location is used only on the phone, to calculate zmanim.
  • Contacts are read only when you ask the assistant to call or message someone.
  • The assistant uses Apple's on-device model; your questions never leave the phone.

Questions or problems? Email kosherswitch@outlook.com or open an issue on GitHub.

Kosher Switch is not a kashrut certification. Parents remain responsible for how a phone is used.
