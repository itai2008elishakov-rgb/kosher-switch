Kosher filter

A kosher filter for the whole phone.

For Android and iPhone · Updated October 2026

The short version: most kosher filters only watch the browser. Kosher Switch turns the whole phone into a kosher phone with one tap: only the apps you choose, no internet except what you allow, and settings locked behind a code that someone you trust keeps.

What is a kosher filter?

A kosher filter limits what a phone can show and do, so it stays kosher. There are a few kinds:

  • Browser filters check websites inside one browser. Another browser or app can go around them.
  • Network filters (a VPN or DNS) block websites for the whole phone, but every allowed app still has its full content.
  • Kosher phones are sold already locked down, but they mean buying, carrying and charging a second phone.

Kosher Switch combines the strong parts: it filters the apps, the internet and the settings of the phone you already own.

What Kosher Switch filters

  • Apps: in kosher mode only the apps you chose are visible. Everything else is hidden.
  • Internet: blocked for every app except the ones you allow, like WhatsApp or Waze.
  • Websites: allowed apps still go through a family filter with SafeSearch. We recommend no browser at all, and that is the default.
  • Pictures: if you do allow a browser on Android, the Kosher Browser covers every picture with a person in it, even on Google Images.
  • Settings: installing, removing, VPN and DNS changes and resets from Settings are locked. Only the code turns the phone back.

A kosher filter for Samsung and other Android phones

Kosher Switch works on any Android phone with Android 10 or newer: Samsung Galaxy (A series and S series), Google Pixel, Xiaomi and Redmi, Motorola, OnePlus and keypad phones like the Qin F21 Pro. Setup is one time, with a QR code or a computer, because Android only lets a filter lock the phone through that special step. See Kosher Switch for Android.

A kosher filter for iPhone

On iPhone, Kosher Switch is built on Apple’s Screen Time, so it needs no computer and no reset. It hides the apps you don’t keep, filters websites in Safari and other apps, and gives you a kosher Home Screen made of widgets. See Kosher Switch for iPhone.

Kosher filter or kosher phone?

You don’t have to choose. In kosher mode your phone is a kosher phone, with a siddur, tehillim, zmanim and a luach built in. When you really need the full phone, the person who holds the code can switch it back. No second phone to buy or carry.

Can it be bypassed?

No filter is 100% bypass-proof, and anyone who says otherwise is overselling. On Android, kosher mode blocks Settings, installing and uninstalling, VPN and DNS changes, safe mode, USB debugging and resets from Settings, and it survives restarts. A full factory reset with the hardware buttons can’t be blocked by any app, but it erases the phone, so it can’t happen unnoticed.

How to set it up

  1. Install Kosher Switch: Android or the App Store.
  2. Choose which apps stay, and let someone you trust choose the code.
  3. Tap the switch. The phone is kosher right away.

Kosher Switch is a tool, not a kashrut certification. For a certified kosher phone, speak to your rav or a recognized kashrut organization.
